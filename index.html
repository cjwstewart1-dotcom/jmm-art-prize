Glasgow · Annual since 2024

An art prize for young people, in memory of a teacher who believed in them

The Jackie Marno-McGoldrick Art Prize is a free annual competition and exhibition for school pupils across West Dunbartonshire, held in Glasgow’s West End. It continues the work of Jackie Marno-McGoldrick, artist and art teacher at Clydebank High School.

The 2026 prize · complete

The 2026 competition was a huge success

The Jackie Marno-McGoldrick Art Prize 2026 took place from 23rd to 29th October at The Alchemy Experiment on Byres Road, and it was a brilliant week. Pupils from Clydebank High School, St Peter the Apostle High School and Dumbarton Academy filled the gallery with around 90 pieces of work, and a packed opening night celebrated the winners alongside their families, teachers and supporters.

We kept the competition true to its roots - free to enter, open to every school pupil across West Dunbartonshire, and built around a real exhibition in Glasgow’s West End - and carried on the amazing work Jackie’s son, Callum Stewart, started in 2024. Thank you to every pupil, teacher, partner and supporter who made it happen.
